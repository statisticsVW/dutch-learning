# Dutch Learning Aid

A self-directed tool that helps one curious young teen teach himself Dutch (Netherlands standard) from scratch, on his own schedule.

## Language

**Learner**:
The one person the aid is built for: a beginner who chose to learn Dutch out of curiosity, not for a class or a trip.
_Avoid_: User, student, player

**Curator**:
The person who shares the aid with the Learner and adds or corrects its content; not a teacher and not required to speak Dutch.
_Avoid_: Admin, teacher, parent, owner

**Burst**:
A practice sitting the Learner starts whenever he feels like it, of any length, with no expected schedule between Bursts.
_Avoid_: Daily session, lesson, streak day

### Content

**Card**:
One Dutch word or short phrase with its English meaning; a noun Card always carries its article (de/het).
_Avoid_: Flashcard, item, word

**Pack**:
A themed group of Cards the Learner can choose to explore (e.g. food, animals, being polite).
_Avoid_: Deck, lesson, unit, category

**Mini-story**:
A short Dutch text built almost entirely from Cards the Learner already knows.
_Avoid_: Reading, passage, lesson

**Cognate**:
A Dutch word an English speaker can already guess because it looks and means the same (appel, water).

**False friend**:
A Dutch word that looks like an English word but means something else (brutaal = cheeky).
_Avoid_: Trap word

**Dictionary**:
The fixed set of common Dutch words bundled with the aid that the Learner can search; a Dictionary entry is not a Card until he adds it.
_Avoid_: Glossary, word list, lookup

**Fact**:
A short "did you know" note about the Dutch language or culture, attached to a Card or Pack.
_Avoid_: Tip, trivia, note

### Between Learner and Curator

**Word request**:
A message the Learner sends the Curator asking for a word that isn't in the Dictionary.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, ticket

**Mistake report**:
A message the Learner sends the Curator flagging a Card or Fact that looks wrong.
_Avoid_: Bug report, flag, feedback
